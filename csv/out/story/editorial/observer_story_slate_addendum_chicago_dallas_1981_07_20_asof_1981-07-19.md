# Baseball Observer Story Slate Addendum — Chicago–Dallas

**Newsroom date:** July 20, 1981  
**Data cutoff:** Games completed through July 19, 1981  
**Status:** Additive annotation; existing slate and rankings unchanged

## Why It Belongs on the Board

Chicago earned a 2–2 road split at Detroit by winning the final two games, including a 4–1 victory on July 19. The Fire now return home for four scheduled games against first-place Dallas from July 20 through July 23.

Chicago begins the series 48–45, third in the NBC Central and four games behind 52–41 Dallas. Because every game is head-to-head, the direct division margin can move sharply: a Chicago sweep produces a tie at 52–45, while a Dallas sweep leaves Chicago eight games back.

The matchup also carries governed historical context. Chicago reached the playoffs five times from 1972 through 1980, including the 1973 Grand Series, without winning a championship. That record adds franchise stakes; it does not prove what the current team feels or intends.

## Why It Does Not Replace Dallas–Detroit

Dallas–Detroit remains the lead because it is the proven top-of-division race: Dallas leads Detroit by two games. Chicago is third and four games back. The Chicago–Dallas series is therefore a strong direct-opportunity setup, not a replacement for the current standings lead.

StatsPlus reinforces that distinction. Dallas carries 60.0% division odds compared with Chicago's 15.0%. Those model estimates enrich the setup but do not replace the official standings or predict the series result.

## What Not to Overstate

- Chicago cannot take sole possession of first place in this four-game series; a sweep can only tie Dallas.
- Do not imply or use a July 20 result.
- Do not call Chicago the model favorite; the StatsPlus odds favor Dallas.
- Do not convert five playoff appearances without a title into a curse, redemption claim, or assumed clubhouse burden.
- Do not infer motivation, fan emotion, manager intent, or clubhouse drama.
- Scheduled starters are unavailable in the approved evidence.
