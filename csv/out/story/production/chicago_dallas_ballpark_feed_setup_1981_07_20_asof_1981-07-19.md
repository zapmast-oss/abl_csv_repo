## Clean Opening Setup

Here’s the skinny: Chicago comes home after salvaging a four-game road split in Detroit, and first-place Dallas is waiting on the schedule for four games at Chicago Grounds.

The Fire lost the first two games in Detroit, then won the final two. Chicago completed the split with a 4–1 victory on July 19. That result leaves the Fire 48–45, third in the NBC Central and four games behind the 52–41 Rustlers.

## The Series

Dallas is scheduled at Chicago on July 20, 21, 22, and 23. These are four direct games against the division leader. No result from July 20 is part of this setup, and scheduled starters are unavailable in the approved evidence.

The series arithmetic gives the matchup its stakes:

- Chicago sweep: both clubs finish the series 52–45, tied in the standings.
- Chicago wins three of four: Chicago finishes two games back.
- Split: Chicago remains four games back.
- Dallas wins three of four: Chicago falls six games back.
- Dallas sweep: Chicago falls eight games back.

Chicago cannot take sole possession of first place during this series. Its maximum direct gain is a tie.

That history gives the series franchise context. It does not establish that current players feel a burden, that the club is seeking redemption, or that four July games will define the season.
