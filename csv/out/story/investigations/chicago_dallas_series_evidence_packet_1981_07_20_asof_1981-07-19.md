**Newsroom date:** July 20, 1981  
**Evidence cutoff:** Games completed through July 19

## Confirmed Facts

- Chicago played four consecutive road games at Detroit, July 16–19.
- Detroit won the first two, 4-2 and 10-0. Chicago won the final two, 4-2 and 4-1.
- Game 1108 on July 19 was the fourth and final game and completed a 2-2 road split.
- Chicago is 48-45 and four games behind 52-41 Dallas; Detroit is 50-43 and two games back.
- Dallas is scheduled at Chicago for four games, July 20–23. Game IDs are 1119, 1131, 1143, and 1155. All are unplayed in the approved cutoff.
- A Chicago sweep would tie Dallas at 52-45. Three Chicago wins would cut the gap to two. A split leaves four; one Chicago win leaves six; a Dallas sweep leaves eight.
- Chicago made the playoffs five times from 1972–1980 and won no championships.
- Chicago’s best finish was a seven-game loss to Denver in the 1973 Grand Series.
