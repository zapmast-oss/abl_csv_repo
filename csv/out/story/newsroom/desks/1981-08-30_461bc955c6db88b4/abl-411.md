# ABL 411 | Around the six divisions

ABL Newsroom | August 31, 1981 | Results through August 30

## NBC Eastern Division

Charlotte’s lead over Miami shrank from 2.5 games to 1.5 games. Charlotte is 73-58 and Miami is 71-59. Miami beat Charlotte 6-5.

On August 31 the schedule lists Atlanta at Dallas, Tampa Bay at Los Angeles, and Miami at Phoenix. With no Charlotte game listed that day, a Miami win would gain half a game on Charlotte.

## NBC Central Division

Dallas (72-59) kept its one-game lead over Chicago (71-60). Dallas beat Detroit 4-2. Chicago beat Minneapolis 5-3.

On August 31 the schedule lists Atlanta at Dallas and Detroit at San Francisco. With no Chicago game listed that day, a Dallas win would gain half a game on Chicago.

## NBC Western Division

Phoenix (70-61) kept its one-game lead over San Francisco (69-62). Los Angeles slipped from 1 back to 2. Phoenix beat San Diego 4-1. San Francisco beat Los Angeles 10-4.

On August 31 the schedule lists Tampa Bay at Los Angeles, Miami at Phoenix, and Detroit at San Francisco. A San Francisco win and a Phoenix loss would bring them level.

## ABC Eastern Division

Boston (68-63) kept its 3-game lead over Philadelphia (65-66). Boston beat Pittsburgh 2-0. Philadelphia beat New York 10-0.

On August 31 the schedule lists Cincinnati at New York and Pittsburgh at Denver. The next change in the margin depends on the next recorded decisions for Boston and Philadelphia.

## ABC Central Division

Houston’s lead over Cincinnati widened from 7 games to 8 games. Houston is 75-56 and Cincinnati is 67-64. Houston beat Nashville 10-9. St. Louis beat Cincinnati 4-2.

On August 31 the schedule lists Cincinnati at New York, St. Louis at Portland, and Nashville at Seattle. With no Houston game listed that day, a Cincinnati win would gain half a game on Houston.

## ABC Western Division

Las Vegas (72-59) kept its one-game lead over Denver (71-60). Seattle slipped from 0 back to 1. Las Vegas beat Portland 5-2. Denver beat Seattle 2-1.

On August 31 the schedule lists Pittsburgh at Denver, St. Louis at Portland, and Nashville at Seattle. With no Las Vegas game listed that day, a Denver win would gain half a game on Las Vegas.
