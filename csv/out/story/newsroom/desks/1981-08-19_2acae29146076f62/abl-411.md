# ABL 411 | Around the six divisions

ABL Newsroom | August 20, 1981 | Results through August 19

## NBC Eastern Division

Charlotte (69-52) kept its 4.5-game lead over Miami (64-56). Minneapolis beat Charlotte 9-3. San Diego beat Miami 6-4.

On August 20 the schedule lists Miami at Atlanta and Charlotte at Tampa Bay. A Miami win and a Charlotte loss would cut the gap to 3.5 games.

## NBC Central Division

Dallas (66-55) kept its one-game lead over Detroit (65-56). Chicago closed from 3 back to 2. Phoenix beat Dallas 3-0. Atlanta beat Detroit 8-6. Chicago beat Los Angeles 10-3.

On August 20 the schedule lists Detroit at Chicago and Dallas at Minneapolis. A Detroit win and a Dallas loss would bring them level.

## NBC Western Division

Phoenix and San Francisco remained tied at 64-57. Los Angeles slipped from 1 back to 2. Phoenix beat Dallas 3-0. San Francisco beat Tampa Bay 18-2. Chicago beat Los Angeles 10-3.

On August 20 the schedule lists San Diego at Los Angeles and Phoenix at San Francisco. The winner of the Phoenix–San Francisco game moves a full game ahead of the loser.

## ABC Eastern Division

Boston’s lead over Philadelphia shrank from 5 games to 4 games. Boston is 61-60 and Philadelphia is 57-64. Nashville beat Boston 6-2. Philadelphia beat Portland 3-1.

On August 20 the schedule lists Philadelphia at Boston and New York at Pittsburgh. A Philadelphia win over Boston would cut the gap to 3 games.

## ABC Central Division

Houston’s lead over Cincinnati shrank from 10.5 games to 9.5 games. Houston is 72-48 and Cincinnati is 63-58. Las Vegas beat Houston 5-4. Cincinnati beat Denver 4-1.

On August 20 the schedule lists St. Louis at Houston (unfinished, 5 innings already recorded), Nashville at Cincinnati, and Houston at St. Louis. If Cincinnati wins all its listed games and Houston loses all of its games that day, the margin falls to 8 games.

## ABC Western Division

Las Vegas’ lead over Denver widened from 2 games to 3 games. Las Vegas is 69-52 and Denver is 66-55. Seattle slipped from 4 back to 5. Las Vegas beat Houston 5-4. Cincinnati beat Denver 4-1. Pittsburgh beat Seattle 4-1.

On August 20 the schedule lists Portland at Denver and Las Vegas at Seattle. A Denver win and a Las Vegas loss would cut the gap to 2 games.
