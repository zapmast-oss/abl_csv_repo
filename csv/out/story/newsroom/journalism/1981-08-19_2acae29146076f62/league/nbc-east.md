# Before Charlotte and Miami meet, the rest of the East gets a say

League desk  |  ABL Newsroom  |  League date 1981-08-20  |  Games through 1981-08-19

## The race before the race

Charlotte and Miami will next meet on August 27 in Miami. There is already a race to determine what that series will be worth. Charlotte's 69-52 record leaves Miami (64-56) 4.5 games back. The margin stood at 8 games before play on July 27.

Miami has done better outside the East, going 45-32 to Charlotte's 41-37. Inside it the order reverses. The Colonels are 28-15 and the Hurricanes 19-24. Returning to division play puts Miami back in the part of its schedule where it has lost ground.

## What Atlanta and Tampa Bay can change

On August 20, Charlotte is scheduled at Tampa Bay and Miami at Atlanta. The Kings are 9-6 against the Hurricanes. The Storm are 4-11 against the Colonels. Miami could win in Atlanta and still gain nothing that day if Charlotte wins too.

The schedule still has eight meetings between the two leaders. In each of those games a Miami win cuts a full game from the gap and a Charlotte win adds one. Until then, Atlanta and Tampa Bay have as much say in how close this race gets as either contender.
