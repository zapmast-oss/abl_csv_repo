# Phoenix visits San Francisco with the NBC West tied

The Baseball Observer  |  Ernie Bewell  |  ABL Newsroom  |  League date 1981-08-20  |  Games through 1981-08-19

## Where the race is closest

Phoenix and San Francisco are tied at 64-57 in the NBC West. A week ago Phoenix trailed the other club by 3 games. Phoenix has gone 9-6 head-to-head. The schedule puts Phoenix at San Francisco on August 20.

San Francisco beat Tampa Bay 18–2 on August 19. Take that game out of San Francisco’s last seven through that date and the other six left the club 1-5, with 14 runs scored and 22 allowed. One game can change the look of a whole week’s run totals.

## Two division leaders, two different margins

Las Vegas beat Houston 5–4 on August 19. Across their three consecutive meetings, Las Vegas went 2-1. The arithmetic matters differently in the two divisions. Houston and Cincinnati are 9.5 games apart, while Las Vegas and Denver are 3 games apart.

## The view from the NBC East

In the NBC East, Charlotte is 69-52 and Miami is 64-56. Charlotte first reached the tournament in 1980. Miami has already been there six times. A return this fall would give Charlotte consecutive appearances for the first time. For Miami, start with where the runs have gone. The club has scored 453 and allowed 478.

## One player to follow

In 2 appearances during Phoenix’s last 7 games, Stephen Grimes worked 16 1/3 innings, allowed 1 earned run and struck out 13. Before those games, his season line for Phoenix included 161 innings and 66 earned runs. How much of Phoenix’s next pitching workload falls to Stephen Grimes?
