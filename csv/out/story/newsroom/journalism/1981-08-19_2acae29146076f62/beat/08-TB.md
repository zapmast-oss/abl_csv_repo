# Nate Smeltz keeps hitting while Tampa Bay pays for the walks

Tampa Bay Storm beat  |  ABL Newsroom  |  League date 1981-08-20  |  Games through 1981-08-19

## What one hitter can do

Nate Smeltz went 1-for-3 in Tampa Bay's 18-2 loss to San Francisco on August 19. Through August 19 he is batting .328 with 21 home runs and 90 RBI. His 154 hits lead the Storm. The harder numbers to turn around belong to the whole club. Tampa Bay went 2-5 over its last 7, scoring 16 runs and allowing 45.

## Two pitchers after a trade

Jonathan Funches walked eight hitters in 2 1/3 innings against San Francisco and was charged with nine earned runs. The walks have followed him since the July 24 trade. For Dallas he walked 57 in 117 2/3 innings. For Tampa Bay he has walked 24 in 32.

The pitcher the Storm sent the other way, Brian Manchester, has allowed one earned run in 31 2/3 innings over five starts for Dallas. For Tampa Bay the immediate question is whether Funches can get through a lineup without handing it so many baserunners. Charlotte comes to town on August 20. The Colonels have already scored 81 times in 15 games against the Storm.
