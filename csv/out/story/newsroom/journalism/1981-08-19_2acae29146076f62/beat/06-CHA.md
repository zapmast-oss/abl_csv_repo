# How Charlotte built a lead it can defend

## A second pennant race

Charlotte spent its first eight ABL seasons outside the tournament. In 1979 the Colonels went 87-75. A year later they went 91-71 and won the East. Now they are 69-52, 4.5 games ahead of Miami. The best evidence that Charlotte can defend its title comes from the clubs that would have to take it away.

The Colonels have gone 28-15 against the three teams trying to catch them and 41-37 against the rest of the league. They have outscored their neighbors 216-149. Charlotte is 8-6 against Miami, 9-5 against Atlanta and 11-4 against Tampa Bay.

Jose Hernandez has worked 66 1/3 innings against East opponents. In nine starts he has allowed 19 earned runs and struck out 61. That comes to a 2.58 ERA against the clubs closest to home.

## Back inside the division

The Colonels are 7-10 in August, with 61 runs scored and 77 allowed. The schedule gives them four games at Tampa Bay, starting August 20. Of Charlotte's three division opponents, the Storm have already allowed the Colonels 81 runs in 15 meetings.
