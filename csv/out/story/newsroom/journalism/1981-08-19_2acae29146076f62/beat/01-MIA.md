## The cost of a bad loss

The Hurricanes have won 64 games and lost 56, even though opponents have outscored them 478-453. The margins help explain how. Miami is 20-16 in one-run games. In decisions by at least five runs it is 10-16, with 102 runs scored and 164 allowed. A lopsided loss does lasting damage to the run differential. It still costs one game in the standings.

## Who has actually played

Daniel Vega had 29 starts and went 29-for-110 with Pittsburgh. Since the July 28 trade he has made 11 starts for Miami, going 17-for-49 with one home run. Addie de Ruijter had seven starts and went 8-for-29 with Phoenix. Since the July 29 trade he has made 17 starts for Miami, going 14-for-56 with two home runs.

Joe Puerta, the pitcher acquired from St. Louis on July 27, has allowed no earned runs in 3 innings for Miami. The Hurricanes were 52-48 before the first of those trades. From July 27 through August 19 they went 12-8, scoring 65 and allowing 59. The two hitters have played enough to change the lineup. How much credit to give the newcomers for the club's record remains open.
