# Atlanta has already shown Miami what it can do

Atlanta Kings beat  |  ABL Newsroom  |  League date 1981-08-20  |  Games through 1981-08-19

## The opponent Miami has to get past

Miami comes to Atlanta on August 20 with four games on the schedule here. The Kings are 55-66, but they have beaten the Hurricanes nine times in 15 meetings and outscored them 57-46. Their last meeting went 11 innings on July 19. Atlanta won 5-4.

Bill Ybanez has given the Kings 30 2/3 innings against the Hurricanes, allowing eight earned runs and striking out 24. In 27 starts this season he has pitched 189 innings. If Miami sees him again, it will face the pitcher who has worked the most innings against it for Atlanta this year.

## What the Kings have at stake

Atlanta went 31-48 through June. Since July 1 it has gone 24-18. Jordan Santistevan has contributed 21 home runs and 83 RBI this season. Over the stretch since July began, the whole club has scored 141 runs and allowed 148. The better record gives the Kings something of their own to defend while Miami plays for position.
