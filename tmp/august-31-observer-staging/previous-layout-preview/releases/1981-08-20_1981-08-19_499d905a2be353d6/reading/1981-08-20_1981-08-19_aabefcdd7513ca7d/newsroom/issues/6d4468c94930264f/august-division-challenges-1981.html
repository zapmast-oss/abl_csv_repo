What the games can move

At the opening gap, even a Miami sweep of Charlotte would leave the Hurricanes half a game behind. If both clubs complete their first six as scheduled, the gap stays in half-game increments. A Miami sweep can put the Hurricanes ahead if they enter the series 3.5 games back or closer. Atlanta and Tampa Bay can help determine that number. Wild card

Miami starts half a game behind Detroit for the wild card. Its four with Charlotte are a route toward the division as well as a chance to gain on teams playing one another in the Central. Keep an eye on both races when Miami and Detroit have played different numbers of games.

Four club notes

Charlotte

The 1980 division winner has held first despite being outscored 77-61 in August. Dabir Aly allowed three earned runs in 15 1/3 innings since August 10. Watch whether the offense gives starts like those room, especially with Santiago Castro 6-for-36 in the same span.

Miami

The club finished 73-89 last year. Daniel Vega hit 13-for-37 for Miami in August after the trade, and Addie de Ruijter had 55 plate appearances. Track the lineups and which newcomer gets the important trips in the Atlanta series.

Atlanta

The Kings have won nine of 15 from Miami this season. Eric Gray homered three times in 37 plate appearances from August 10-19. Atlanta beat Detroit 8-6 on the cutoff day and hosts the East's top two clubs before visiting Tampa Bay.

Tampa Bay

Tampa Bay was 4-11 against Charlotte through the cutoff and allowed 88 runs in 17 August games. Frank Jimenez went 12-for-29 from August 10-19. Jonathan Funches' outings give the Storm a second story to track after the July starter swap.

Five leading storylines

1. Phoenix and San Francisco go straight to the question of first

The two are tied after San Francisco held a six-game lead. The first game settles their head-to-head position for the night, and four games can separate them by four. After the series, see whether the difference came from repeated run creation, pitching, or a single outsized game. Keep Los Angeles' record alongside theirs.

2. The NBC Central runs through Chicago

Chicago hosts Detroit four times and Dallas twice before Detroit visits Dallas. The Fire enter two off first and one off the wild-card pace. Follow all three clubs' finals to see whether Chicago's six home games change the division lead or move the Fire into wild-card position.

3. Philadelphia's four games at Boston are a direct test

Four back and four road games against first place. The Fury can draw level with a sweep. After August 23, check whether their pitching and run support carried over from one game to the next and whether Pittsburgh closed on either club.

4. The ABC West asks Seattle two separate questions

Can Seattle cut Las Vegas' division margin, and where will it be relative to Denver when their four begin? Keep Denver's two in between against Las Vegas on the same board. Check how much of the movement came from the clubs meeting directly.

5. Miami needs to bring a smaller number home

At 4.5 back, four wins alone against Charlotte leave Miami half a game short. Miami's first six games and Charlotte's road games at Tampa Bay and Atlanta will determine the gap entering the finale. If the first six are complete as scheduled, a gap of 3.5 or less lets a four-game Miami sweep overtake Charlotte.
