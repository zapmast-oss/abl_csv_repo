# Seattle saw both sides of a one-run finish

Seattle Comets beat  |  1981-08-31  |  Games through 1981-08-30

Adam Wilson singled in the 14th inning August 21, scoring David Olguin to beat Las Vegas 4–3. Seattle took three of four from the Gamblers and both games at Portland. It shared first before the final day. Then Denver won the finale 2–1, and Las Vegas finished its own trip with a win.

Tony Miller struck out eight in 7⅓ innings in that last loss. Carlos Merlos had 17 hits during the challenge. Seattle went 7–3 and made up four games on Las Vegas. Losing the last two let Denver catch it.

Nashville comes in on August 31. Denver and Seattle share the wild-card line at 71–60, and Las Vegas still has to come back to Seattle for three September 28–30. The Comets get time to get ahead before the next couple of one-run games carry the same kind of price.
