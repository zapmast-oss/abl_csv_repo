Each conference sends three division winners and its best remaining club. If the season ended on these records, Charlotte would be the NBC’s top seed and Miami its wild card. The same-division protection would pair Charlotte with Phoenix and Dallas with Miami. In the ABC, Houston would face the Denver–Seattle wild-card survivor, with Las Vegas facing Boston. The August standings establish position. The remaining schedule and any official tiebreak determine the field.
