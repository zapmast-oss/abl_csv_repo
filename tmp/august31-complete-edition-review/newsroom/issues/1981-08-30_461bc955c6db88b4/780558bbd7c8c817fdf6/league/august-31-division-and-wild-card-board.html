Wild cards and the DCS under the 1981 rules

Each conference sends three division winners and its best remaining club. Miami is 71–59, half a game ahead of Chicago (71–60), two and a half ahead of San Francisco (69–62), three and a half ahead of Los Angeles (68–63), and five and a half ahead of Detroit (66–65). Detroit held the wild-card position on August 19.

Denver and Seattle are tied at 71–60 in the ABC, four games in front of both Cincinnati and St. Louis and six ahead of Philadelphia. Denver held the wild-card position alone on August 19. The standings supply no official decision between Denver and Seattle.

If the season ended on these records, Charlotte would be the NBC’s top seed and Miami its wild card. The same-division protection would pair Charlotte with Phoenix and Dallas with Miami. In the ABC, Houston would face the Denver–Seattle wild-card survivor, with Las Vegas facing Boston. The August standings establish position. The remaining schedule and any official tiebreak determine the field.
